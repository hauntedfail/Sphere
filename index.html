Sphere

Set up an IQOS ILUMA from this page over Bluetooth. No account, no app, and nothing leaves your browser.

Before you connect

  1. Wake the device: open the charger lid, or press its button.
  2. Close the IQOS app. The device accepts one connection at a time.
  3. The first time, your system asks to pair. Accept it.

What stays on this page
  • Everything. The page's security policy forbids network requests, so nothing can be sent anywhere.
  • Saved device labels live in this browser's storage and nowhere else.
  • The serial number is never read. Browsers block it, and Sphere does not need it.
